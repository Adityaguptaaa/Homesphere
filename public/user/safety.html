🛡️ Safety Tips

Before Service

  • • Always check helper's verification badges
  • • Share service details with a trusted contact
  • • Set a safe word with family members

During Service

  • • Keep your phone charged and nearby
  • • Allow helper to work in common areas when possible
  • • Share live location with emergency contacts

If You Feel Unsafe

  • • Immediately press the panic button
  • • Call emergency services (100)
  • • Use your safe word in chat to alert our team
  • • Leave your home if possible and go to a public place
